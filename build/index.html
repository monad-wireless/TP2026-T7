01 / THE PROJECT

A different way
to understand a space.

MONAD-SEC investigates real-time Wi-Fi Channel State Information (CSI) for occupancy and intrusion sensing on the faculty floor. We aim to connect network measurements, signal processing, and machine learning to detect presence and movement.
