Adding a writeup​

Copy templates/meeting-template.md into docs/meetings/ and use a descriptive filename such as YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md. Replace every template field, add the document ID to sidebars.js, and link the writeup in the table above.

Each writeup should capture:

  • Date, location or meeting format, and attendees.
  • Agenda and progress since the previous meeting.
  • Discussion summary and decisions, including their rationale.
  • Action items with owners and due dates.
  • Open questions and the next meeting date, if confirmed.

Publish only the version approved for sharing. Do not include credentials, private meeting URLs, or personal information that is not intended for publication.
