How we work

Status: proposed working approach, based on the team's motivation letter. Final assignments and meeting cadence will be confirmed by the team.

Two connected workstreams​

WorkstreamFocus
Hardware & networksHardware evaluation, network setup, low-level programming, and signal acquisition.
Data & artificial intelligenceData preparation, processing pipelines, model development, and evaluation.

The proposed split is one group of three and one group of four. Each group would have a lead, and one member would coordinate the overall project. No individual appointments are implied here.

A repeatable cycle​

  1. Plan: describe the problem, owner, expected output, dependencies, and acceptance criteria on the project board.
  2. Investigate and implement: work in small, reviewable changes; record assumptions and experimental conditions.
  3. Review: have another member review changes and check documentation and relevant tests.
  4. Evaluate: compare results with agreed criteria, record limitations, and identify the next experiment.
  5. Share: capture decisions and follow-up actions in the meeting notes.

Coordination​

Both workstreams should agree on data formats and interfaces early. Cross-team reviews and shared integration checkpoints should keep acquisition and analysis aligned. The proposed coordinator would track dependencies, progress, and communication across the groups.

Proposed definition of done​

  • The task's acceptance criteria are met and reviewed.
  • Relevant code, configuration, or experimental procedure is reproducible.
  • Tests or measurements appropriate to the change are recorded.
  • Documentation reflects the result, including known limitations.
  • Follow-up work has an explicit owner or is entered in the backlog.

Decisions and experiments​

Document the context, considered options, choice, and consequences of important decisions. For experiments, record the hardware, environment, dataset version, preprocessing, parameters, and evaluation method. Avoid reporting unverified results as established capabilities.
