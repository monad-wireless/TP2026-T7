# MONAD-SEC

**Team 07 · FIIT STU, Bratislava · Academic year 2026/2027**

## Research direction

Our topic is **real-time Wi-Fi CSI occupancy and intrusion sensing for the faculty floor**. We are interested in using Wi-Fi Channel State Information (CSI), Doppler effects, and potentially additional sensors to investigate the presence or movement of people in a room.

This website documents our intended direction. It does not claim a working deployment or measured detection accuracy. The homepage signal explorer is a conceptual illustration, not a live sensor feed.

## What we plan to explore

1. **Acquire signals:** investigate suitable hardware, network configurations, and low-level collection of CSI measurements.
2. **Prepare data:** clean measurements, document collection conditions, and develop repeatable processing pipelines.
3. **Build and evaluate models:** investigate methods for detecting occupancy and movement; compare accuracy, robustness, and computational cost.
4. **Integrate the system:** connect acquisition and inference and evaluate feasibility for faculty-floor sensing.

Technology choices, evaluation targets, and deployment details remain to be agreed and recorded as the project progresses.

## Team strengths

The seven-member team combines experience in SQL and NoSQL databases, embedded systems, FPGA and microcontrollers, networking, infrastructure, cybersecurity, data engineering, machine learning, and data visualisation. Five members study ISS and two study IB.

These are collective skills described in the team brief; individual project roles have not yet been assigned.
